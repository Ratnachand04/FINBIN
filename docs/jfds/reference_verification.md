# Reference and journal source checks

Checked against publisher, author-hosted or institutional primary sources while preparing the revised manuscript (22 September 2026 UTC). This is not an exhaustive systematic literature review. No citations were added solely to increase reference count.

- Jaquart, Dann and Weinhardt (2021), *Short-term bitcoin market prediction via machine learning*, **The Journal of Finance and Data Science** 7, 45–66. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfds.2021.03.001 . Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405918821000027 . Author institutional full text: https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000150665/149388567 . Used only to situate short-horizon Bitcoin prediction; not evidence for this study's numerical results.
- Gradojevic, Kukolj, Adcock and Djakovic (2023), *Forecasting Bitcoin with technical analysis: A not-so-random forest?*, **International Journal of Forecasting** 39(1), 1–17. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2021.08.001 . Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169207021001230 . The article's volume year is 2023 although the DOI contains 2021.
- Arnott, Harvey and Markowitz (2019), *A Backtesting Protocol in the Era of Machine Learning*, **The Journal of Financial Data Science** 1(1), 64–74. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3905/jfds.2019.1.064 . Author manuscript: https://people.duke.edu/~charvey/Research/Published_Papers/SSRN-id3275654.pdf . The correct DOI does not repeat the issue number, and this journal is not the target journal.
- Kaufman, Rosset, Perlich and Stitelman (2012), *Leakage in data mining: Formulation, detection, and avoidance*, **ACM TKDD** 6(4), Article 15, 21 pages. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1145/2382577.2382579 . Publisher fetch was restricted; metadata and the author's earlier conference paper were cross-checked. Do not confuse the three-author 2011 conference version with the four-author 2012 journal version. Earlier author paper: https://www.cs.umb.edu/~ding/history/470_670_fall_2011/papers/cs670_Tran_PreferredPaper_LeakingInDataMining.pdf .
- Newey and West (1987), **Econometrica** 55(3), 703–708. DOI: https://doi.org/10.2307/1913610 . Author/institutional working paper and published-version metadata: https://www.nber.org/papers/t0055 . Primary PDF: https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/t0055/t0055.pdf .
- Diebold and Mariano (1995), *Comparing Predictive Accuracy*, **JBES** 13(3), 253–263. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/07350015.1995.10524599 . Primary paper hosted by a university: https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~behansen/718/DieboldMariano1995.pdf . Used to motivate paired loss differences, not to claim an exact finite-sample DM test.
- Politis and Romano (1994), *The Stationary Bootstrap*, **JASA** 89(428), 1303–1313. DOI/publisher: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476870 . The paper's weak-stationarity/dependence conditions are not a guarantee against crypto regime changes.
- Jia and Harman (2011), **IEEE TSE** 37(5), 649–678. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TSE.2010.62 . Author-hosted accepted manuscript: https://crest.cs.ucl.ac.uk/fileadmin/crest/sebasepaper/JiaH10.pdf . Used for mutation-testing background, not for the study's mutation score.
- Binance public data documentation: https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/ . Official source of field definitions, archive conventions and the switch to microsecond timestamps for spot files from January 2025. The selected raw archive URL and SHA-256 check for every sampled month are in `source_spot_check.json`.

Official target-journal guide: https://www.keaipublishing.com/en/journals/the-journal-of-finance-and-data-science/guide-for-authors/ . Reviewed for editable LaTeX sources, double-anonymized review, author information, competing-interest uploads, and AI disclosure/human accountability.
